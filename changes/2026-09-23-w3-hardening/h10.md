# H10: observability contract

Batch H10 of the [W3 hardening](intent.md): the W0-10 log line schema, readiness enforced on pending migrations, and the actor on `request.completed`. Synthetic data only.

## Findings addressed

1. **Duplicate `time` key and numeric `level` on every line** (runtime-ops, bug). Pino wrote its own epoch `time` and numeric `level` next to the emitter's ISO `time`, against the [W0-10 §3.2](../../docs/engineering/observability-contract.md#32-line-schema) line schema. `loggerOptions` now sets a `timestamp` that writes one ISO `time` and a `formatters.level` that writes the label. `createEmitter` no longer passes the line's own `time` to pino. The returned `LogLine` keeps its `time` for callers. `http.test.ts` asserts `'info'`/`'warn'` levels and exactly one `"time":` per raw line. Before the fix the levels were `[30, 40, 30]`. No consumer parsed the numeric level: `tests/support/`, the desk-health view and `w3-int-log-coverage` never read `level`.
2. **Readiness reported but never enforced** (runtime-ops, bug). `buildApp` adds an `onRequest` hook after the drain and correlation hooks and before authorization. While `readiness().store.migrations === 'pending'` it answers every route except `/healthz` and `/readyz` with 503 `internal_error`, and that includes the SPA shell. The hook reads the same 5-second cached reader as `/readyz` (`createReadinessReader`), so a burst costs one probe. Tests: a new `http.test.ts` case sends a burst of five requests and gets five 503s from one probe. `/healthz` stays 200 and `/readyz` reports `pending`. The real-process OBS-03 test in `w3-int-observability.test.ts` now also gets 503 `internal_error` on `/api/cases` against an unmigrated database, with one correlated `request.completed` line. Before the fix it got 401. [W0-10 §5.2](../../docs/engineering/observability-contract.md#52-fail-closed-rule) now states this rule.
3. **`request.completed` never carried the actor** (runtime-ops, test-gap). The `onResponse` hook reads the request context's `actor`, which the authorization middleware sets. It adds `actorSubjectId`, plus `actorRole` when the actor holds exactly one role. Both fields were already registered in the §3.3 catalogue. A new `http.test.ts` case covers a signed-in request. `context.ts` is unchanged.

**Mail readiness under `local-google` (recorded, not changed).** `start.ts` binds a mail sink only in fixture mode. Under `local-google`, `mailSink.status` is therefore `unavailable` and `/readyz` answers 503 by design. This stays until W0-10 adds a "not configured" mail status. The new gate does not depend on overall readiness, only on `store.migrations`, so a `local-google` process still serves its routes.

## What got smaller or clearer

- `http.test.ts`: one `readinessOf(probes)` helper replaces the two copies of the `computeReadiness` configuration. One `identityResolving(resolve)` helper replaces the inline fake identity. Both are used by more than one test.
- The log line has one `time` and one level vocabulary, the one the spec already documented, so W8's collector needs no remapping.
