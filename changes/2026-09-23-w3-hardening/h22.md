# H22: Shared integration app harness, part 1: composeAppDeps plus the W2 suites

## Findings addressed

Both findings still applied on main `e8efb73`.

- **Integration app wiring pasted into 9 files and drifting from start.ts (architecture simplify, medium; part 1).** Each W2 suite repeated about 100 lines of `rebuildApp` and the before/beforeEach/after reset, and none of them matched `start.ts`: no readiness or operator routes, no `readyRecipientsForOwner` in three of the four, no `uploadTrigger`, and pack limits cut down to one field.
  - The new `server/src/compose-app-deps.ts` exports `composeAppDeps({ config, db, adapter, fixtureUsers, businessUnits, store, qcRunner, mailSink, readiness, now, webDistDir })`. It returns the `AppDeps` that `startServer` used to build inline, and `startServer` now calls it. It takes no `knownIdentities` input: the fixture identities are derived from `fixtureUsers`, as they were in `start.ts` after H8.
  - The new `tests/support/fixture-app.ts` exports `openFixtureApp({ now, qcRunner? })`. It builds the fixture identity adapter and a readiness reader, calls `composeAppDeps` with the test database, the blob store and `FIXTURE_USERS`, and builds the app through the observed-app wrapper. It installs the shared before, beforeEach and after hooks (open the database and blob directory; reset, `TRUNCATE session, registry_counter`, blob reset, `loadFixtures`, rebuild; close). It also exports the live `app`, `db`, `capture` and `diagnostics` bindings and `rebuildApp`, `signIn`, `submit`, `submitOk` and `caseRevision`. `qcRunner` is a factory, so each rebuild gets a fresh scripted runner, as each suite did before. `rebuildApp(null)` binds no runner and `rebuildApp(runner)` binds another one.
  - `w2-02`, `w2-04`, `w2-05` and `w2-06` now use it. Each keeps only its own settings: the clock start, the QC runner (unscripted, all fixture cases, or vendor only) and, in `w2-05`, the audit and log checks it adds to `submitOk` and the no-leak `afterEach`. The inline no-runner rebuild in `w2-05` (45 lines) is now `rebuildApp(null)`.
  - **Where the harness still differs from production.** It removes `versions.qc` from the composed deps. `start.ts` always binds a background submit-QC run: with no runner, that run records `unavailable not_configured`. These suites never had that run. They drive submit QC by hand through `runAndPersistSubmitQc`, and a background run would race their `qc_run` assertions (for example, "one unbound run", or the late-QC test's zero runs). Every other group is the production composition. `TESTING.md` records this. The harness readiness reports the mail sink as `ok`, as `w2-05` did before, because no sink is bound in-process.
  - Clock: fixtures now load one minute before the app clock instead of the suite advancing its clock one minute after loading. Each suite's start time is its old start plus one minute, so every timestamp is the same as before.
  - Part 2 (H23) moves `w3-01`, `w2-03`, `w2-09`, `w3-05` and `w3-03a`. `mailSink` is not an option yet. It has no user in these four suites, so it will be added with `w3-03a`.
- **start.ts threads `now` and `db` into every deps group with 8 conditional spreads (architecture simplify, low).** `AppDeps` now has optional top-level `db` and `now`. `buildApp` defaults `now` to the wall clock once and injects `db` and `now` into every `register*` call and the submit trigger. The group types omit `db`, `now`, `emitter` and `errors` (`Omit<…, Injected>`), and `IdentityDeps` loses `now`. `start.ts` has no conditional spreads left for the deps (it passes `qcRunner`, `mailSink` and `fixtureUsers` possibly undefined). Substrate tests that register no database-backed group still build without `db`. A group registered without `db` now fails at build with `buildApp: a route group needs deps.db`. Before, it built routes that failed at their first query.
  - Every other `buildApp` caller moves `db` and `now` to the top level (w1-01, w1-02, w1-03 helpers, w1-04, w1-05, w2-01, w2-03, w2-09, w3-01, w3-03a, w3-03b, w3-05, w3-hardening-idempotency-race, `observability/http.test.ts`). Some suites had given groups different clocks. With one clock per app, the whole app now reads the suite clock. This covers the artifact routes in w1-04, w1-05, w2-01, w2-03, w2-09, w3-01, w3-05 and w3-hardening, and the in-app notifications in w3-03a; both had used the wall clock. In w3-03b's restarted app, identity, pack and versions now read the digest clock. No assertion changed. In `w3-03a` the suite's own dispatcher also moves to the app clock (`createNotifications({ ...deps, db, now, … })`): its `Ready … retry waits for its deadline` test failed (attempts 2, expected 1) while the app dispatcher ran on the frozen clock and the suite's dispatcher on the wall clock.
