# Specification

Source: W4b plan section 15 row W4-06d and sections 3.1-3.3 (decisions 10, 22, 28 and 30). The plan wins over issue #244.

Done when:

1. **`PACK-CONTRADICTION`** (`server/src/qc/content/rules/pack-contradiction.ts`), trigger `submit` only. For each fact of `params.facts` (in order), the rule collects the grammar claims (plan 3.2) of the artifacts whose slot is in that fact's `slots` whose item (`params.items`, keyword lists keyed by fact ID) is the fact and whose answer normalises to `yes` or `no` (`na` and `unknown` state nothing). When two **different artifacts** state the fact with different answers, the fact contradicts: one finding. Agreeing facts, facts stated by one artifact only, and facts no artifact states raise nothing. Two answers inside one artifact are not a pack contradiction.
2. **Finding shape.** Scope `{ kind: 'pack' }`; `claimKey` = the fact ID (decision 30), so `findingKey` = `PACK-CONTRADICTION:pack:<fact>` and two contradicting facts are two findings; owning lane = the pack lane of the request's lane mapping (`ai_coe`, W0-06 7.1); severity from the catalogue (`medium` in the seed); `measure` null. Evidence = exactly two entries, request (slot) order: the first statement in request order, then the first statement of another artifact with the other answer; each with the artifact reference, the claim's locator and `excerptHash`. Message `qc.finding.pack_contradiction` with params `{ fact, slotA, slotB }` (a key and two slot numbers). Passes `validateQcFinding` with the request's artifacts and `checkOwningLane` with run lane null.
3. **Params.** `QC_RULE_PARAMS_SCHEMAS` gains `PackContradictionParamsSchema`: `slots` (content slots, never 9), shared `labels`, `items` (a record keyed by fact ID `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,63}$`, bilingual keyword lists), `facts` (1-16 `{ id, slots }` with at least two slots each), `claimSource`; additional properties refused. A cross-field check (`packContradictionParamsProblems`) refuses a fact listed twice, a fact without keywords, keywords for no fact, and a fact slot outside `params.slots`; `qcRulesBodyProblems` reports it on publish, and the content runner treats it as invalid params (`runner_error` / `invalid_rule_params`) before reading a byte.
4. **Seed.** Both templates list `PACK-CONTRADICTION` (content, `['submit']`, medium) after the `ACC-*` rules with `PACK_CONTRADICTION_PARAMS` (`slots [2, 5]`, facts `personal_data` and `external_vendor` on slots 2 and 5, English and Thai keywords, `claimSource 'grammar'`). Label stays `w4a.1`; "every seed body validates" stays green. It is not routed by `model_type`.
5. **Runner.** `CONTENT_RULES` lists the rule. On submit the runner reads slots 2 and 5 (plan 3.1); an unreadable slot 2 or 5 makes the submit content part unavailable (`artifact_unreadable`), never clean.
6. **Registry (W6-03 merged).** `IMPLEMENTED_RULES` lists `PACK-CONTRADICTION` as `content`, `['submit']`; a server unit test asserts the registry's `content` entries equal `Object.keys(CONTENT_RULES)`, trigger for trigger.
7. **Locales.** `qc.finding.pack_contradiction` and `qc.rule.pack_contradiction` in th and en (sorted).
8. **Cross-check.** On the W4-09a dev split, the content runner reproduces exactly the labelled findings of every implemented content rule on every trigger including submit (`ev-dev-14`: one, `ev-dev-15`: two), and the W4-08a harness shows no part mismatch.
9. **Documents.** A dated W4-06d note on W0-07 3.5 (the rule row).
10. The plan's full gate (section 16) is green.
