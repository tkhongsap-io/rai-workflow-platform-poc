# Intent: register risk_rubric, seed the synthetic placeholder, rubric read endpoint (W5-02, #213)

W5-01 defined what a `risk_rubric` configuration body may contain and the engine that scores it, but nothing could publish, seed or read a rubric yet. This ticket makes the rubric real configuration: `risk_rubric` becomes a registered kind, validated on publish by its schema and by `riskRubricBodyProblems`, exactly as `qc_rules` is; the configuration seed publishes revision 1 of a clearly labelled **SYNTHETIC PLACEHOLDER** rubric (label `synthetic-placeholder.1`); and `GET /api/configuration/risk-rubric/current` returns the rubric in force now, or 404 when none is.

Because the seed now holds the kind, every new submit freezes the placeholder's revision in `frozen_configuration` next to the other kinds; `configuration_revision_id` stays the `qc_rules` revision. Nothing scores yet (W5-05), nothing stores answers yet (W5-04) and nothing is shown yet (W5-07).

It is order 2 in section 9 of the [W5 plan](../../docs/engineering/implementation-plan-w5.md) and implements sections 2, 3 ("Placeholder seed") and 6 (the rubric read) under the register rows "Ta's delegation (2026-09-27)" and "W5 delegated rulings (provisional)" (R-1, R-2, R-7, R-9, R-16's server half). D07 (the questionnaire's content, thresholds and labels) stays open for AI/COE: every seeded value is a placeholder, its thresholds are deliberately unlike the operating-model section 7 summary, and the schema still accepts only `provenance: 'synthetic_placeholder'`. Synthetic data only; no network call; no deploy.
