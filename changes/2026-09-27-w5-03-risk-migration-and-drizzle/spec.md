3. **Tests** (`tests/integration/w5-03-risk-migration.test.ts`, real Postgres, fixture set):
   - updating `risk_answers` on a submitted version raises `rai.frozen_version` (as `rai_app` and as `rai_owner`); on the open draft it succeeds; a draft's `risk_answers` defaults to `{}`;
   - an UPDATE or DELETE on `risk_proposal` raises (`rai.append_only`, or `42501` for DELETE as `rai_app`);

   - a second submit proposal on one version violates the unique index; a `recheck` proposal on the same version is accepted;
   - the status consistency CHECK refuses a `proposed` row without a tier or rubric revision and an `unavailable` row with a tier.
