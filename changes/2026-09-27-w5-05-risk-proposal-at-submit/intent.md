This ticket records the desk's risk proposal at the moment a version is submitted: inside the submit transaction, as a pure function of the frozen answers, the frozen `risk_rubric` revision and the frozen slot states (R-4). Every submitted version gets exactly one proposal row the moment reviewers can see it. A missing rubric, an invalid frozen rubric or an engine failure records an `unavailable` proposal with its reason and leaves `risk_tier` NULL; the submit still commits. The tier is a proposal, not a governance decision: it opens, skips or routes nothing (all three lanes open for every tier) and the Ready predicate does not read it.
