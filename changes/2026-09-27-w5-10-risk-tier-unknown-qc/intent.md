# Intent: RISK-TIER-UNKNOWN QC input (W5-10, #240)

W5-05 records a risk proposal on every submit, but QC does not see it. A version whose proposal is `unknown` (questions unanswered or without evidence) or `unavailable` (no rubric, invalid rubric, engine error) can reach Ready without anyone having looked at the missing risk evidence. The W5 plan (R-11) makes the proposal a soft QC input: such a version gets one soft finding, `RISK-TIER-UNKNOWN`, owned by AI/COE, which AI/COE confirms or waives before Ready like any other soft QC finding (L7). A `high`, `medium` or `low` proposal adds nothing, and no tier grants, skips or routes anything.

This ticket carries the proposal to the QC boundary as a required request field (R-17), adds the metadata rule to the deterministic runner and the seeded `qc_rules` catalogue (label `w5.1`), and updates every request builder and every existing test the W5-10 row names. It is order 7 in section 9 of the [W5 plan](../../docs/engineering/implementation-plan-w5.md) (the W5-10 row) and implements section 8 ("QC input (W5-10)"), decisions R-11 and R-17, under the register rows "Ta's delegation (2026-09-27)" and "W5 delegated rulings (provisional)". The rule list and severity stay provisional until D09. D07 stays open (the rubric is the SYNTHETIC PLACEHOLDER). No migration. Synthetic data only; no network call; no deploy.
