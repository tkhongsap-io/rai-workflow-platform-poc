# Intent: ACC-BAND-V1-SHEET3 (W4-06c, #236)

W4-06a and W4-06b gave QC a content runner, the provisional synthetic claim grammar and three content rules. The seeded `llm` and `other` approve-attempt catalogue of template `v1.0 Sheet3` still lists `ACC-BAND-V1-SHEET3`, which the runner cannot execute, so it refuses such a request (`unknown_content_rule`).

This ticket adds that rule: the source spec's go-live hallucination bands of checklist template v1.0 Sheet 3 (SL#2.1: high-risk tier below 1%, medium below 2%, low below 3%). A hallucination-rate figure stated in the AI/COE risk-screening document (slot 1) that is at or above the band of its stated tier is a high-severity finding; a figure with no recognisable tier is a finding too, because the band cannot be applied. Percent and ratio figures are both accepted, and the comparison is exact (ratio × 100 as decimals, never floating point), so a figure equal to the band fails, as "below" requires.

The bands belong to v1.0 Sheet 3 only (L12): `v2.0` never selects the rule (the seed omits it and publishing refuses it there), and the rule itself raises nothing on any other template. It reads slot 1 on the AI/COE approve attempt only (decision 28). Its params (tiers, bands, metric, labels) are catalogue data with a registered schema, seeded on the `v1.0 Sheet3` template, so the seed still validates and the D09 owners can retune them.

With this rule in place, every content rule the seeded approve-attempt catalogue lists is implemented, so a seeded `llm` or `other` approve attempt runs end to end on the content runner. It is W4b ticket 14 (track T4, rules) and implements the [W4b plan](../../docs/engineering/implementation-plan-w4b.md) section 15 row W4-06c and sections 3.1-3.3 under the register rows "Ta's delegation (2026-09-27)" and "W4b delegated rulings (provisional)". The runner stays unbound in every `QC_MODE` until W4-13b. D07-D10 stay open; the band values, tier words and metric list are provisional until D09 (WA-D09). Synthetic data only; no network call; no migration.
